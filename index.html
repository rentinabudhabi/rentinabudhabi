FAQ

What is the total estimate?

Everything you pay over the first year, added up as follows:

Rent — the full advertised annual rent. Splitting it into cheques changes when you pay; though paying by cheque typically adds a one-off cheque fee of around AED 500, which is included in the estimate, and some owners may increase the total rent for a higher number of cheques.

Projected TAQA / Tasleem bills — the municipality fee at 5% of the annual rent, plus roughly estimated monthly utilities, each paid across twelve monthly bills (total = one year's worth of both).

Adjacent one-off expenses — the TAQA and Tasleem deposits (AED 2,000 combined), the security deposit (5% of rent, or 10% for a furnished unit), the AED 500 cheque fee when paying by cheque, the moving expenses when included, misc one-off expenses, Tawtheeq/admin costs, and the 5% agency commission when using an agency.

Refunds are not netted out: deposits count in full as cash you hand over — the refund expectations are detailed in the items below.

What is the security deposit, and can I get it back?

Paid up front and held by the owner or agent — typically 5% of the annual rent for an unfurnished unit, and typically 10% for a furnished one (the calculator switches automatically with the Furnished option). Don't count on the full amount coming back: even for an undamaged unit, the owner usually keeps something for maintenance and repairs — often in the range of AED 1,000–2,000 — and that deduction is often unconditional. The exception: only if the owner agrees to it, so negotiate the refund terms and get them in writing before signing.

What about furnished units?

When the unit is already furnished, the furnishing is typically included in the advertised rent, so you don't budget for buying furniture — but expect a higher security deposit (typically around 10% instead of 5%) since the landlord also holds you accountable for the furniture's condition. The kept amount may vary, and does so more widely for furnished units.

What are the TAQA / Tasleem deposits?

Two mandatory utility guarantees, AED 1,000 each (AED 2,000 combined here): TAQA covers your electricity, water — and optionally gas, if you connect it. Tasleem is the district-cooling (AC) deposit, which is mandatory when you rent. Both are refundable when you close the accounts and move out.

What if I cancel before the contract ends?

TAQA bills are charged irrespective of your rental contract — cancelling it early still requires paying for the entire year. Expect the final bill to be summed against your deposit in one closing settlement: depending on how far into the contract you are, you either get part of the deposit refunded, or you need to pay extra.

On the rental side, early termination is governed by your contract: many contracts carry a penalty of up to two months' rent, and typically require an upfront notice of 2–3 months. This varies contract by contract, so always check the termination clause before signing.

What is Tawtheeq?

The official tenancy contract registration in Abu Dhabi. If you rent directly from an owner you can register it yourself for around AED 50. Property management companies typically don't give you the option to register it yourself, so their one-off fee of roughly AED 600–1,500 should be considered an unavoidable cost. The calculator defaults to an average of about AED 800.

What is the municipality fee?

A fee of 5% of the annual rent, charged monthly through your utility bills — so it scales with your rent, not your consumption. The bills pace it as follows: the first month's bill covers only the usage, the second month's bill adds the first two months' fee on top of the usage, and from the third month on every bill is one month's fee plus the usage.

How accurate is the utilities estimate?

It is a rough guess, shown as a range: the monthly bill is estimated at 3.6%–4.8% of the monthly rent. Heavy air-conditioning use in summer pushes you toward the upper end. Replace the range with your contract's real recurring bills once known.

Is the agency commission included?

Only if you select “Using an agency”, at 5% of the annual rent. Renting directly from the owner saves this cost; many listings advertised as “no commission” still have it baked into the rent.
